# Phishing Awareness Training

## Objective
To educate users about phishing attacks and how to protect themselves from online scams.

## Topics Covered
- What is Phishing?
- Types of Phishing Attacks
- How to Identify Phishing Emails
- Fake Website Detection
- Social Engineering Techniques
- Prevention Tips

## Outcome
Created an awareness presentation explaining phishing attacks, their risks, and best practices to stay safe online.
